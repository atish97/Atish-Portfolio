CryptoPulse Asset Tracker

Follow portfolio performance, market movements, and digital asset activity through a focused mobile experience.

Role UX/UI Designer
Industry Fintech & Web3 Mobile
Duration 2.5 months

Stage 1: Discover & Research

Reviewed feedback from everyday crypto investors who felt overwhelmed by cluttered trading apps full of noisy tickers and confusing charts. Users just wanted a calm, trustworthy place to check their total balance, daily gains, and coin news.

Stage 2: Define & Ideate

Focused the experience on clarity and speed. I stripped away unnecessary trading clutter from the home screen and built a clean portfolio overview with interactive timeframe scrubbing and simple profit and loss breakdowns.

Stage 3: Design

Wireframing: Mapped out the onboarding wallet sync, main portfolio feed, asset detail view, and custom price alert setup on mobile screens.

UI Design & Components: Crafted crisp number typography, gentle green and red trend indicators, and modular asset cards that stay readable even during fast market moves.

Interactive Prototype: Built smooth touch gestures for scrubbing price history charts, switching currencies, and pinning favorite tokens to the top watchlist.

Stage 4: Test & Iterate

Tested the mobile prototype with ten casual and active investors. Users appreciated how easy it was to read their net allocation at a glance and asked for a quick privacy toggle to hide balances in public spaces.

Stage 5: Implement & Handoff

Collaborated with iOS and Android developers to fine tune chart touch targets, haptic feedback points, and smooth screen transitions.

Key Outcomes

Beta testers checked their portfolio more consistently and rated the app significantly easier to understand compared to traditional exchange apps.
