Frequently Asked Questions

As a UX Engineer, I work across both design and development. I handle the process from understanding the requirements and designing user experiences to building functional interfaces. This allows me to bring ideas from the initial concept to a working product.

I always try to keep my pricing reasonable and flexible. Every project is different, so my rates depend on the project requirements, complexity, and overall scope. The best way to get an accurate estimate is to share your ideas with me so we can discuss the details.

I usually start by understanding the project, its goals, and the people who will use it. From there, I explore ideas, map out user flows, create wireframes, and gradually move into high fidelity designs. Once everything feels right, I move towards development, testing, and refining the final experience.

I'm naturally drawn to clean and minimal design. I believe a good interface should feel simple without sacrificing functionality. I pay close attention to user flows and always look for ways to reduce unnecessary steps, remove friction, and make the overall experience more intuitive.

Absolutely. I enjoy working on both new ideas and existing products. Whether it's improving an existing user experience, refining interfaces, fixing usability issues, or introducing new features, I can help make the product more intuitive and easier to use.
