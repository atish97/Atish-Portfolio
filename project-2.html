Procurement Operations Dashboard

Track purchase requests, supplier activity, approvals, and spending across procurement workflows.

Role UX/UI Designer
Industry B2B SaaS & Supply Chain
Duration 4 months

Stage 1: Discover & Research

Talked with finance managers and procurement coordinators to see why purchase approvals were getting delayed. Most teams were chasing updates across emails and spreadsheets because the existing dashboard buried pending requests under too many nested menus.

Stage 2: Define & Ideate

Designed a role focused home view that brings pending approvals, budget limits, and active vendor deliveries right to the top. The goal was to let approvers review context, check remaining department budget, and approve or request changes in under thirty seconds.

Stage 3: Design

Wireframing: Mapped out the request to approval journey, splitting complex purchase forms into quick scannable sections with smart vendor defaults.

UI Design & Components: Created clean spend summary cards, supplier scorecards, and filterable ledger tables with clear visual hierarchy so numbers stay easy to read.

Interactive Prototype: Built a working prototype covering purchase request creation, multi step manager sign off, and real time budget variance alerts.

Stage 4: Test & Iterate

Tested the redesigned flows with eight procurement specialists and finance reviewers. Based on their input, I added a quick comparison drawer so managers can view past vendor quotes side by side before approving a new order.

Stage 5: Implement & Handoff

Partnered with the engineering team during sprint handoff to refine data table sorting, keyboard navigation, and responsive layouts for tablet approvals on the go.

Key Outcomes

Average purchase approval time dropped from three days to just a few hours. Finance teams gained clear visibility into monthly department spend without needing manual spreadsheet exports.
